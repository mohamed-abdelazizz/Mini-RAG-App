MOHAMED SALAH: BIOGRAPHY AND CAREER OVERVIEW

EARLY LIFE AND BEGINNINGS
Mohamed Salah Hamed Mahrous Ghaly was born on June 15, 1992, in Nagrig, a village near Basyoun in northern Egypt. Growing up with a deep passion for football, he began his youth career with the Egyptian club El Mokawloon (Arab Contractors). As a teenager, he famously endured grueling daily commutes—often taking multiple buses for hours each way—to attend training sessions in Cairo. His dedication paid off when he made his senior debut in the Egyptian Premier League in 2010.

RISE IN EUROPE
Salah's standout performances earned him a move to European football in 2012 when he signed with Swiss club FC Basel. In Switzerland, he quickly gained international attention for his blistering pace, dribbling, and eye for goal, winning consecutive Swiss Super League titles.

In early 2014, he transferred to English side Chelsea. Although playing time at Stamford Bridge was limited, subsequent loan spells in Italy with Fiorentina and AS Roma revitalized his career. At Roma, Salah blossomed into one of Serie A's most dangerous forwards, prompting Liverpool to sign him in the summer of 2017.

LEGENDARY ERA AT LIVERPOOL (2017–2026)
At Liverpool, Salah became a global superstar and earned the nickname "The Egyptian King." Across nine seasons at Anfield, he shattered numerous club and Premier League records, scoring 257 goals and providing 120 assists in 442 appearances across all competitions.

During his time with the Reds, Salah won multiple Premier League Golden Boots, two PFA Players' Player of the Year awards, and every major club trophy available—including the UEFA Champions League (2019), the Premier League title (2020), the FIFA Club World Cup, the FA Cup, and the EFL Cup. He concluded his storied Liverpool tenure in May 2026 as the club's all-time leading Premier League goalscorer and the highest-scoring foreign player in Premier League history.

MOVE TO TURKEY (2026–PRESENT)
In August 2026, Salah embarked on a new chapter by signing a two-year contract with Turkish Süper Lig club Trabzonspor. Making an immediate impact in Turkey, he quickly established himself at the top of the league's scoring charts, including a memorable hat-trick against Galatasaray early in the 2026–27 campaign.

INTERNATIONAL CAREER WITH EGYPT
As captain and talisman of the Egypt national team, Salah has carried the hopes of his nation for over a decade. He famously scored the decisive penalty that sent Egypt to the 2018 FIFA World Cup, ending a 28-year absence from the tournament, and later captained the Pharaohs to the Round of 16 at the 2026 FIFA World Cup. He has also led Egypt to multiple Africa Cup of Nations finals. In November 2026, Salah reached 70 international goals, cementing his legacy as Egypt's all-time leading goalscorer.

OFF THE PITCH
Beyond football, Salah is celebrated across the Middle East and the world for his humility and extensive philanthropy. Through the Mohamed Salah Charity Foundation, he has funded schools, hospitals, clean water facilities, and financial support programs for families in his hometown of Nagrig and across Egypt.
